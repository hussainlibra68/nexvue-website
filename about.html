Made to be lived with.

VERAFIL began with a simple frustration: homes full of things that looked good for a photograph and nothing else. We design furniture, lighting and textiles that get better with use — in warm woods, honest metals and soft natural fibres.

Our approach

Every piece is made in small runs with partner workshops we know by name. We choose reclaimed, recycled and responsibly sourced materials wherever we can, and we price fairly by selling directly to you.

Materials

Reclaimed oak and solid walnut. Recycled stainless steel and spun brass. Organic cotton, wool and stonewashed linen. Natural materials vary — every piece is a little different.
