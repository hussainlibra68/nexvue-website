Optilax Drive

Optilax Drive is tuned specifically to cut glare from oncoming headlights, reducing halo and starburst effects during night driving.

Benefits

Why Optilax Drive

Headlight Glare Control

Reduces halo and starburst effects from oncoming headlights.

Sharper Night Contrast

Keeps road markings and signage easier to read after dark.

Reduced Eye Fatigue on Drives

Less glare means less squinting on long or frequent drives.

How It's Applied

Optilax Drive builds on the standard AR stack with an additional layer calibrated to the specific wavelengths emitted by LED and halogen headlights, targeting the glare sources most relevant to driving rather than general ambient light.
