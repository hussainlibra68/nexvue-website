Stock & Availability

The NexVue range below is available for standard stock ordering. For custom prescriptions outside these ranges, get in touch and we'll confirm availability.

NexVue SV — Single Vision

Everyday Clarity, Simplified

Our entry-level single vision lens, ground and finished for accurate, dependable correction across the full field of view.

Advantages and Benefits:
  • Accurate power correction edge to edge
  • Lightweight, comfortable base curve
  • Available in standard indices
[REPLACE image: NexVue Pro packaging / product shot]

NexVue Pro — Premium Single Vision

Sharper by Design

A digitally surfaced upgrade to standard single vision, cutting peripheral distortion for noticeably sharper edge-to-edge clarity.

Advantages and Benefits:
  • Digital surfacing for edge-to-edge sharpness
  • Reduced peripheral distortion
  • Ideal for higher prescriptions
[REPLACE image: NexVue Progress packaging / product shot]

NexVue Progress — Progressive

No Line, No Compromise

A no-line progressive design blending near, intermediate and distance vision into one lens with a smooth, wide corridor.

Advantages and Benefits:
  • Seamless near-to-distance transition
  • Wide, usable intermediate zone
  • No visible dividing line
[REPLACE image: NexVue Blue packaging / product shot]

NexVue Blue — Blue Light Filter

Built for Screen Time

Filters blue-violet light from phones, laptops and LED lighting to help ease digital eye strain during long screen sessions.

Advantages and Benefits:
  • Filters blue-violet screen light
  • Reduces digital eye strain
  • Available with UV protection
[REPLACE image: NexVue Photo packaging / product shot]

NexVue Photo — Photochromic

Adapts as You Move

Clear indoors, darkens automatically in sunlight — one lens that adjusts to changing light without switching glasses.

Advantages and Benefits:
  • Automatically adjusts to UV light
  • Fully clear indoors
  • Built-in UV protection outdoors

NexVue AR+ — Anti-Reflective

Clearer Vision, Clearer Look

Our anti-reflective finish cuts glare from headlights, screens and overhead lighting while keeping the lens nearly invisible.

Advantages and Benefits:
  • Reduces glare and reflections
  • Improves night driving visibility
  • Easier to keep clean

NexVue Drive — Driving / Glare Control

Made for the Road

Tuned specifically for time behind the wheel, cutting halo and glare from headlights while keeping road detail sharp after dark.

Advantages and Benefits:
  • Reduces headlight glare and halo
  • Sharper night-driving contrast
  • Comfortable for long drives
