How Orion Works

Using high-index materials combined with digital surfacing, Orion reduces lens edge thickness for higher powers while keeping the optical centre precisely aligned to the wearer's visual axis.

Advantages and Benefits:
  • Noticeably thinner edges on strong prescriptions
  • Sharper central and peripheral vision
